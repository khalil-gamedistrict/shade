# Shade — Kahani

Apocalypto film ke dhaanche se mutasir (inspired). Naam, kirdar aur scenes hamare apne hain.

Jungle mein ek chhota sa qabeela hansi khushi reh raha hai. Ek din **jangli shikaariyon ka gol** un pe
hamla kar deta hai — sab jaan bacha kar bhaagte hain. Hamara hero un shikaariyon se bachne ke liye
jungle, aag, darya, toofan aur chattanon se hota hua bhaagta hai.

Kahani bina alfaaz ke dikhayi jaati hai (Limbo ki tarah): silhouettes, awaazein, mausam.

## Kirdar (silhouette)
- **Qabeela:** gaon ke mard, auratein (lambe baal, ghaghra), bachche — trailer mein alaao ke gird.
- **Hero:** lambe khule baal (daurte waqt peeche udte), kaan mein gol baali, langot, nange paon.
- **Shikaari (3):** pankhon ka taaj + neza · sar pe juda + gol dhaal + neza · kalgi + kaman + tarkash.
  Patli ghusse wali aankhen.

## Peechha (har chapter mein)
- Shikaari peeche daurte aate hain — ruke ya ghalti ki to pakar lenge.
- Neze phenkte hain (kaman mein) — chalte raho ya koodo.
- Hilte platformon wale bare gaddhay paar nahi kar sakte — kinare se neze phenkte hain, phir doosre raaste se aate hain.
- **Har chapter ke aakhir mein panah:** zameen mein patton se dhaka ek gaddha — hero us mein gir kar neeche ki
  khokhli jagah mein chhup jata hai (aankhen band, saans roke). Shikaari daurte hue theek us ke oopar aa kar rukte
  hain, idhar udhar dekhte hain (chhat se mitti jharti hai), phir us ke oopar se guzar kar aagay bhaag jaate hain.
  Hero aankhen kholta hai: *"Safe — for now."*
- **Jhukna** (↓ / S / neeche ka button): hero ghutnon ke bal jhuk kar aahista chalta hai. Seene ki oonchai pe aate
  neze sar ke oopar se guzar jaate hain. Har chapter mein ek **gira hua bara darakht** — tana do darakhton ke
  dushakhon mein phansa, kood ke paar nahi, sirf neeche se jhuk kar. Shikaari bhi jhuk kar guzarte hain.
- Pakre jaane pe shikaari akele hero ko ghere mein lete hain — ek saamne, ek ghoom kar peeche se, kaman wala thora door.
- Awaaz: jang ki cheekh se shuru, dhol jo qareeb aate hi tez ho, beech beech mein cheekhein, hero ki tez saans.

## Chapters

20 active chapters, **10 "sets" ki shakal mein**: har set mein 2 chapter taqreeban barabar
mushkil, phir agla set un se mushkil-tar. Ek akela aadmi jungle mein shikaariyon se bachte
hue survive kar raha hai — pehla hissa jungle (Apocalypto jaisa), doosra hissa Limbo se mutasir
hoke ghane andhere jungle aur phir ek purani kharaba hui basti/khandar ki taraf badhta hai.

ooncha-neecha hai (teelay, naale, gehre gadhe, chattanen).

**Mushkil set ke hisaab se barhti hai:** har set mein shikaari taqreeban ek jaisi raftaar se
(speed/qareebi/neze), phir agla set un se mushkil-tar — Set 1 sab se aasaan, Set 10 (19–20) sab
se mushkil (shikaari ki raftaar World ki raftaar ke bohot qareeb). Khatron ki tadaad bhi isi
tarah set-dar-set barhti hai. Test is tarteeb ki hifazat karta hai.

### Shelved — Chapter "Home"

Ek tajrubaati chapter (`home()` function, `lib/game/levels.dart`) tha: subah ka rangeen jungle
(baaqi sab kaale-safed), sunehri dhoop, phool, titliyan, saaf naala, aakhir mein qabeela neze
utha kar khara hota hai aur shikaari laut jaate hain. Abhi active list mein nahi — zaroorat
padi to baad mein wapas shamil kiya ja sakta hai. Filhaal game Chapter 20 "Homecoming" ke
baad generic "He made it home. THE END" card pe khatam hota hai.

## Trailer (18 s + title)
1. Gaon mein sukoon — *They lived in peace.*
2. Andhere jungle mein jhuke, dabe paon aate shikaari — *But the forest was watching.*
3. Hamla, sab bhaagte hain — *Then the hunters came.*
4. Jalta gaon — *The village burned.*
5. Darya ki taraf — *Run. Don't look back.*
6. Toofan aur bijli — *Through the storm.*
7. Zameen ke neeche chhupna, shikaari oopar — *Hide. Survive. Get home.*

## Maut
Gayab nahi hota: jhatka → girna (jis taraf se waar hua) → zameen pe tarapna → saakit.
Gaddhay mein girne pe haath paon chalaata neeche jata hai, paani mein aahista doobta hai.

## Rukawatein kahani mein

| Game mein | Kahani mein |
|---|---|

| Checkpoint lamp | Raaste ki nishaniyan, ghar ki taraf |

## Hadd
Andhera aur dar haan, lekin dikhaya hua khoon nahi (sab umar ke liye).
